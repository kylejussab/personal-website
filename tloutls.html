Project Overview

Using a shared deck, with no deck building, both player and opponent fight over limited card choices. There are two stages of play, Character and Support, with the latter allowing the player to influence or change the game based on the cards played by the opponent. The project features two distinct play modes: a linear narrative campaign following June Ravel through battles against curated, pre-designed opponents, and a survival mode titled "The Last Stand" which has you battle random enemies with random modifiers for as long as your health allows.
